Tell Us About Your Project

This form helps us understand your business, your current process, the problem you want to solve, and what you expect from the automation. The more details you provide, the better we can understand your requirements and determine the right approach for your project.

What Happens After You Submit?

01 — Confirmation Email

Immediately after submitting the form, you’ll receive a confirmation email letting you know that we’ve received your inquiry.

Note: Sometimes the confirmation email may be filtered into your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder. If you don’t see it in your inbox, please check those folders.
02 — We Review Your Requirements

We’ll carefully review the information you submitted, understand your current workflow and requirements, and identify the most suitable automation approach for your project.

03 — Project Assessment

Based on your requirements, we’ll determine the workflow, integrations, tools, complexity, and other technical details needed to complete the project.

04 — Proposal, Pricing & Agreement

Within 24 hours, we’ll get back to you with the relevant project details, including the proposed solution, pricing, payment information, agreement, and any other important documents or information required before starting.

05 — Project Confirmation

Your project is considered confirmed only after the proposal and agreement are accepted and the required initial payment is completed. We’ll then proceed with the agreed automation work.
