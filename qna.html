Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about our AI workflow development, architecture standards, data safety, and delivery timelines.

We specialize in end-to-end operational workflows: automated customer support triage and AI reply drafting, multi-agent research pipelines, CRM lead enrichment and syncing (HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable), automated invoice parsing, custom Slack and Discord bots with LLM routing, database migrations, and bespoke REST/GraphQL API bridges.

Our primary workflow orchestrator is n8n (both self-hosted on Docker and n8n Cloud), backed by Python and Node.js for custom scripting. On the AI side, we leverage Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, OpenAI GPT-4o, and open-source models via Ollama. For databases, we commonly integrate with PostgreSQL, Supabase, Airtable, and Redis.

Most targeted, single-system automations (such as support ticket ingestion or lead qualification) are scoped, built, tested, and handed over within 3 to 7 business days. Larger enterprise pipelines involving multiple data sources and complex multi-agent reasoning typically take 2 to 3 weeks.

Security and privacy are paramount. All workflows are built directly inside your company's own infrastructure or cloud instances. We never store client API credentials on third-party servers. When configuring LLM integrations, we strictly use enterprise zero-data-retention endpoints that guarantee your proprietary data is never used to train external models.
